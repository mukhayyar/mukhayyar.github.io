      (04)Field notes · Fig. 01CanSat Competition 2023

      A $15 antenna, a 4 km link.

      We needed a reliable link between a falling payload and the ground station. Stock antennas topped out around 3 km. So we built a Moxon–Yagi: two bent-aluminum Moxon rectangles — one as the reflector, one as the driven element — plus five straight dipole-style directors, all taped to a PVC boom — and held a 4.2 km link with 99% of packets received in flight.

      Slide the CanSat along the rail: the meter reads the free-space link budget at 915 MHz.

      Radio
      XBee-PRO 900HP · 24 dBm
      Antenna
      2 Moxon + 5 directors · ≈ 11 dBi
      Field test
      4.2 km · 99% packets
