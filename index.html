(05)FAQStill unsure? Ask me

Questions people usually ask.

What kind of projects are a good fit?

New products that need to reach users fast, AI features for products already in use, and older apps that need a modern stack. If the product touches hardware — sensors, devices, radios — even better.

How do we start?

Book a free 30-minute intro call. If it’s a fit, you get a short written plan with scope, milestones and a price. Work starts when you approve it.

How does pricing work?

The prices above are starting points. Fixed-scope work gets a fixed quote after the intro call; ongoing work is billed monthly. Prices are in USD and exclude third-party costs such as hosting and APIs.

You’re in Indonesia — how does remote work go?

I work from Surabaya (UTC+7) with clients anywhere. My day overlaps with Asia and Australia, and with Europe in the afternoon. Updates are written, so nothing waits on a meeting.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code lives in your repository from day one, and everything built for you is yours once it’s paid for. Happy to sign an NDA before we talk details.

Can you also do the hardware side?

Yes. I’ve written flight firmware for CanSats and rockets, built ground stations and a 4 km radio link, and run computer vision on a Raspberry Pi. I also build rekafab, a PCB design studio in the browser.
